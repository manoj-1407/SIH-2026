01 · RECOVERY PRINCIPLE

Deleted doesn't mean gone.

Standard OS file deletion merely marks filesystem inodes as free. Actual file clusters persist in unallocated space until overwritten. Our engine carves raw byte signatures and performs bounded gap-scan reconstruction on fragmented streams.
